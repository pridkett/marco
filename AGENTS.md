# Commit messages

Always use Conventional Commits. Write a specific, imperative subject in the
form `type(scope): concise description`. The scope is optional.

Every commit must have a body after a blank line. Briefly explain what changed
and why in plain, natural language. Keep the body to one or two short paragraphs
and no more than 200 words.

End every commit message with this exact trailer, separated from the body by
a blank line. The trailer does not count toward the body limits.

```text
Co-Authored-By: OpenAI Codex <noreply@openai.com>
```
